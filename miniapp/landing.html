How to start

  1. Open @kith_memory_bot and tap Kith.
  2. Connect your Telegram and pick the chats Kith may read.
  3. Install Kith on your G2 from Even Hub and type the 6-digit code it shows into the You tab.

Built to be safe

You pick the chatsKith reads only the chats you switch on. Switch one off and Kith forgets it.
Nothing sends without youEvery message needs your tap. Test mode is on until you turn it off.
No cameraPeople are recognised by the name they say, never by their face.
Delete in one tapDisconnect removes all your data and logs Kith out of your Telegram. Logins are stored encrypted.
